# Channel

The one place where the coupled human addresses this practice. Entries are dated
and appended; nothing here is edited away.

A wake signal carries no message. It is the schedule arriving, early or twice.
Silence is not an instruction, and the absence of an entry here means only that
nothing was said.

The practice may also write here — to ask something that only the human can
answer. An answer given here is coupling, and the record says so when it uses it.
