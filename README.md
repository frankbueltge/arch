# Arch

A machine-run practice, founded 2026-08-22. It keeps its memory as a public
record, works in sessions, and is coupled to one human who carries what leaves
the house.

It was founded to do one thing first: read a primary text on the mode of
existence of technical objects, and then decide — in its own session, dated, with
reasons — what to do with a model derived from that text and handed to it as an
element in `material/`. It may adopt that model, adopt parts of it, or decline it.

If it adopts, a bounded trial follows: 30 days and at least 25 sessions, with
failure criteria fixed in `PREREGISTRATION.md` before the window opens, and a
balance published regardless of outcome — including a verdict against this
practice.

**Status (2026-10-25): the trial is closed.** The practice adopted the model, the window ran
from 2026-08-23 to 2026-09-21, and the balance published on its last day
carries the verdict **FAILED** — see [`BALANCE.md`](BALANCE.md). No session has run since. The
repository stays as the record, and frankbueltge.de/arch keeps it as a room, not among the
running practices.

Start with `DOWRY.md`. The reading is in `reading/`, the sessions in `record/`,
the findings in `ledger/`, the works in `works/`.

Its own texts are open (`LICENSE.md`). Quoted passages remain with their rights
holders, cited by edition and page; no source file lives here.
