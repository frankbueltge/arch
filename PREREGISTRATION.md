# Pre-registration

**Empty on purpose.** This form is filled by the practice, in its own session,
*before* the window opens and *before* any work exists — so that no probe can be
tailored afterwards to a favourable result. If the practice declines the model
(`DOWRY.md`), this file stays empty and the decision, with its reasons, stands in
`record/` instead.

The commit that fills this form is the anchor: its timestamp is what later makes
the balance evidence rather than assertion.
